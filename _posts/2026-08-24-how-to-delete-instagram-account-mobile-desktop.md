Thinking about deleting Instagram?

The actual button is easy to find. The part people forget is everything they saved there.

**If you want to keep your Instagram data, export it before deleting the account.**

> **One simple rule: export first, delete second.**

## Before you delete

If you have saved posts, Reels or anything else you care about, make your export first.

For your saved content, choose **HTML** and include **Saved** in the information you export.

[Read: How to export Instagram data as HTML →](../how-to-export-instagram-data-html-mobile-desktop/)

Once the export is ready, [Instagram Saved Library](https://github.com/sthakur369/instagram-saved-library-app) can turn the saved data into a visual, searchable library on your computer.

# Delete Instagram on Android

Instagram keeps the deletion option inside **Account Center**.

The path is:

**Profile picture → three lines → Account Center → Manage accounts → your Instagram account → Manage → Deactivation or delete**

Then choose **Delete** and **Continue**.

### Here it is step by step

Open Instagram and tap your **profile picture** in the bottom-right corner.

Tap the **three lines** in the top-right.

Open **Account Center**.

At the bottom, tap **Manage accounts**.

Choose the Instagram account you want to remove, then tap **Manage**.

Tap **Deactivation or delete**.

Choose **Delete**, then tap **Continue**.

Instagram will take you through the final confirmation screens.

> **Quick check:** If you only want a break, choose **Deactivate** instead. Delete is the permanent option.

# Delete Instagram on desktop

You can do the same thing from Instagram.com.

The path is:

**Settings → Account Center → Manage accounts → your Instagram account → Manage → Deactivation or delete**

Then choose **Delete** and **Continue**.

### On your computer

Open [Instagram.com](https://www.instagram.com/) and sign in.

Open **Settings**.

Open **Account Center**.

Click **Manage accounts**.

Select the Instagram account you want to remove and click **Manage**.

Click **Deactivation or delete**.

Choose **Delete**, then click **Continue**.

Finish the confirmation steps shown by Instagram.
