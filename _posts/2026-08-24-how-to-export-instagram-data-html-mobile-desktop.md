---

# Export Instagram data as HTML on Android

The route is:

**Profile picture → three lines → Account Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information → Create export**

Then choose your account and export it to your device.

### On your phone

Open Instagram and tap your **profile picture** in the bottom-right corner.

Tap the **three lines** in the top-right.

Open **Account Center**.

Tap **Your information and permissions**.

Tap **Export your information**.

Tap **Create export**.

Select your Instagram account.

Choose **Export to device**.

Now set the export options:

- **Media quality:** High
- **Format:** HTML
- **Customize info:** select **Media** and **Saved**

Then tap **Start export**.

That's it. Instagram will prepare the export.

> **Don't delete your account while you're waiting for the export.**

---

# Export Instagram data as HTML on desktop

On a computer, the route is:

**Settings → Account Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information → Create export**

### On Instagram.com

Go to [Instagram.com](https://www.instagram.com/) and sign in.

Open **Settings**.

Open **Account Center**.

Click **Your information and permissions**.

Click **Export your information**.

Click **Create export**.

Select your Instagram account.

Choose **Export to device**.

Set:

- **Media quality:** High
- **Format:** HTML
- **Customize info:** select **Media** and **Saved**

Then click **Start export**.

Now Instagram will prepare the files.

## Why choose HTML?

If you just want to read your exported information yourself, **HTML is the simple option**.

You can open the files in a browser.

The catch?

It doesn't look like Instagram.

You may end up with folders of HTML pages and links instead of the familiar saved-post screen.

**That was the annoying part for me.**

So I built [**Instagram Saved Library**](https://github.com/sthakur369/instagram-saved-library-app) to turn the saved information from the export into a visual, searchable library on your computer.

## When the export is ready

You'll get a ZIP file.

Download it.

Extract it.

Then look for your **`saved`** folder.

That's where the saved information lives.

And if you're doing all of this because you're about to delete Instagram, don't stop at:

> “I downloaded my data.”

Open the exported files and make sure you can actually access the things you wanted to keep.

**That's the difference between downloading your data and actually keeping what mattered.**
