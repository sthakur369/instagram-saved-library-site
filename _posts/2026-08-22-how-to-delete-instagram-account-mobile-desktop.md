Thinking about deleting Instagram?

**Before you tap Delete, there is one thing worth doing first: export anything you want to keep.**

Your saved posts and Reels are often the part people remember too late.

> **Export first. Delete second.**

## Before deleting Instagram

If you want to keep your Instagram data, export it before deleting your account.

For saved posts and Reels, include **Saved** in your export and choose **HTML**.

[**How to export your Instagram data as HTML →**](../how-to-export-instagram-data-html-mobile-desktop/)

Once your export is ready, you can use [**Instagram Saved Library**](https://github.com/sthakur369/instagram-saved-library-app) to turn the saved data into a visual, searchable library.

# How to delete Instagram on Android

**Profile picture → three lines → Account Center → Manage accounts → your Instagram account → Manage → Deactivation or delete → Delete → Continue**

**1. Open your Instagram profile**  
Tap your **profile picture** in the bottom-right corner.

**2. Open the menu**  
Tap the **three lines** in the top-right corner.

**3. Open Account Center**  
Tap **Account Center**.

**4. Open Manage accounts**  
At the bottom, tap **Manage accounts**.

**5. Select your Instagram account**  
Choose the Instagram account you want to delete, then tap **Manage**.

**6. Open Deactivation or delete**  
Tap **Deactivation or delete**.

**7. Delete the account**  
Choose **Delete**, then tap **Continue**.

Follow the remaining instructions Instagram shows on screen.

> **Be careful:** Deactivate and Delete are not the same thing. If you only want a break, don't choose Delete.

# How to delete Instagram on desktop

**Settings → Account Center → Manage accounts → your Instagram account → Manage → Deactivation or delete → Delete → Continue**

**1. Open Instagram**  
Go to [Instagram.com](https://www.instagram.com/) and sign in.

**2. Open Settings**

**3. Open Account Center**  
Click **Account Center**.

**4. Open Manage accounts**  
Click **Manage accounts**.

**5. Select your Instagram account**  
Choose the account you want to delete, then click **Manage**.

**6. Open Deactivation or delete**  
Click **Deactivation or delete**.

**7. Delete the account**  
Choose **Delete**, then click **Continue**.

Finish the confirmation steps Instagram gives you.

## Delete or deactivate?

| You want to... | Choose |
|---|---|
| Take a break | **Deactivate** |
| Leave Instagram permanently | **Delete** |

**Not sure? Export your data first. You can decide later.**

## What about your saved posts and Reels?

This is the part that started this whole project for me.

I had years of saved posts and Reels. I could export my Instagram data, but the result was mostly **HTML files and links** — not a nice library I could browse.

That's why I built **Instagram Saved Library**.

It turns the saved information from your export into a **private, searchable visual library** that stays on your computer.

[**Open the free app on GitHub →**](https://github.com/sthakur369/instagram-saved-library-app)

### Before you delete

- [ ] Export the data you want to keep
- [ ] Check that the download is ready
- [ ] Make sure you have your saved content
- [ ] Decide between **Deactivate** and **Delete**
- [ ] Then delete the account

**Small step. Much less regret.**
