Thinking about leaving Instagram?

Deleting the account is the easy part. **Making sure you don't lose something you meant to keep is the part worth doing first.**

If you're ready to permanently delete Instagram, this guide walks you through the current steps on both your **phone and computer**.

> **Important:** If you want to keep your Instagram data, request an export **before** you delete your account. Instagram says that after deletion you won't have access to its export tool.

## Before you delete Instagram

Take a minute before you press the final button.

### 1. Export anything you want to keep

Go to **Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information** and request a copy of your Instagram data. You can choose what information to export and select **HTML** as the format if you want an easy-to-read offline copy.

If your main concern is your saved posts and Reels, make sure you include **Saved** in the information you request.

If you're using Instagram Saved Library, this is the point where you can preserve your saved-content information before leaving.

**[Get Instagram Saved Library →](https://github.com/sthakur369/instagram-saved-library-app)**

### 2. Make sure you really want deletion

Instagram also offers **Deactivate account**, which temporarily hides your profile, photos, comments and likes until you log back in.

Deletion is different: it starts the permanent deletion process.

## How to permanently delete Instagram from your phone

These steps apply to the Instagram app on **iPhone and Android**. The exact screen layout can vary slightly between app versions, but the account-deletion path is handled through **Accounts Center**.

### Step 1 — Open your Instagram profile

Open Instagram and go to your **profile**.

### Step 2 — Open the menu

Tap the **☰ Menu** button in the top-right corner.

### Step 3 — Open Accounts Center

Tap **Accounts Center**.

If Instagram first shows **Settings and activity**, open Accounts Center from there.

### Step 4 — Open Personal details

Inside Accounts Center, tap **Personal details**.

### Step 5 — Open account ownership settings

Tap **Account ownership and control**.

### Step 6 — Choose Deactivation or deletion

Tap **Deactivation or deletion**.

Instagram will show the account or accounts connected to your Accounts Center.

### Step 7 — Select your Instagram account

Tap the Instagram account you want to delete.

### Step 8 — Choose Delete account

Select **Delete account**, then tap **Continue**.

Be careful here.

**Delete** is permanent. **Deactivate** is the temporary option.

### Step 9 — Follow the confirmation screens

Instagram may ask you to choose a reason, enter your password, or confirm the deletion.

Follow the on-screen instructions until the deletion request is submitted.

**That's it.**

You don't need to hunt through old Instagram settings or use a third-party deletion website.

## How to permanently delete Instagram from a computer

You can also delete your Instagram account from a desktop browser. Instagram's current help instructions use **Accounts Center** for this too.

### Step 1 — Open Instagram

Go to **Instagram.com** and log in to the account you want to delete.

### Step 2 — Open More

In the left sidebar, click **More**.

### Step 3 — Open Settings

Click **Settings**.

### Step 4 — Open Accounts Center

Click **Accounts Center**.

### Step 5 — Open Personal details

Click **Personal details**.

### Step 6 — Open account ownership settings

Click **Account ownership and control**.

### Step 7 — Open Deactivation or deletion

Click **Deactivation or deletion**.

### Step 8 — Select your Instagram account

Choose the Instagram account you want to delete.

### Step 9 — Choose Delete account

Click **Delete account**, then click **Continue**.

### Step 10 — Finish the confirmation

Follow Instagram's remaining prompts to confirm the deletion.

Instagram says the deletion request can be canceled during the applicable cancellation period; the Help Center currently states that after **30 days** the account and information are permanently deleted, while noting that the cancellation period can vary by region.

> **One last check:** Before you close the tab, make sure you have already downloaded the Instagram export you wanted to keep.

## Delete or deactivate Instagram?

Not sure which button you need?

| If you want to... | Choose |
|---|---|
| Take a break from Instagram | **Deactivate account** |
| Hide your profile temporarily | **Deactivate account** |
| Leave Instagram permanently | **Delete account** |

Deactivation hides your profile and content until you log back in. Deletion starts the permanent deletion process.

### A good rule

**If you're unsure, don't delete yet. Export first. Decide second.**

That one small step can save you a lot of regret later.
